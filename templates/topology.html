Component Details

  • SDN Controller: Centralized brain that dictates routing paths and isolates threats.
  • Customer / Payment Gateway / Bank Server: Legitimate simulated financial nodes.
  • Attacker: Simulated hostile node launching malicious traffic.
  • Honeypot: The primary threat detection mechanism designed to capture unauthorized access.
